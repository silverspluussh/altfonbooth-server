You’ve got questions, we’ve got answers.

  • How do I get a SIP account?
    Getting started is easy! Simply sign up for an Altfon Booth account and log in. From your dashboard, navigate to the SIP Authentication Accounts section and click "New Account" to create your first SIP credential.
  • How do prepaid credits work?
    Prepaid credits are assigned directly to your individual SIP accounts. You can top up any specific account by clicking "Buy Credits" next to it in your dashboard. These credits are used to fund your outgoing calls and active services.
  • What are Destinations?
    Destinations are the phone numbers or endpoints where your calls are routed. You can easily add, update, or remove destinations for each of your SIP accounts through the management hub.
  • Can I manage multiple accounts?
    Absolutely. Altfon Booth is designed for scalability. You can create and manage multiple SIP accounts under a single master profile, making it perfect for both individual and business use.
