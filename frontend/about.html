About Us

Our Story

Altfon Booth was born from a vision to make advanced VOIP communication accessible, affordable, and easy to manage. We specialize in creating high-performance SIP networks that serve schools, homes, and individuals, ensuring that staying connected is never a challenge.

School Solutions

We provide dedicated phone booth infrastructure for educational institutions, prioritizing safety and accessibility for students.

Home Connectivity

Our residential VOIP services bring crystal-clear voice quality to your home at a fraction of the cost of traditional providers.

Mobile Flexibility

Take your SIP account anywhere. Our personal VOIP solutions work seamlessly on mobile devices for total freedom.

Making it all make sense.

Navigating the world of VOIP can be complex, but Altfon Booth simplifies it for you. We provide intuitive management tools and robust hardware solutions so you can focus on the conversation, not the configuration.

Your Vision, Our Future.

We are constantly evolving to meet the communication needs of tomorrow. By listening to our users, we build features that empower educators, homeowners, and professionals to communicate without boundaries.
